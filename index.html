01 — The service

Three steps. Nothing to install.

If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can order gas. No app, no account, no card details held on file.

I

Send a message

Tell us the cylinder size and your estate. Any day, at any hour. A person replies, not a bot loop.

II

We confirm the price

The price is fixed for the month and identical for every customer. You approve before anything is dispatched.

III

It arrives, you pay

The cylinder arrives sealed and weighed. M-Pesa the rider on the doorstep once you're satisfied.

03 — For dealers

You keep the gas. We bring the orders.

Gas Poa is a demand and delivery layer, not a competing depot. Orders from Nairobi estates come to your counter; we handle the customer relationship and the last mile.

You retain your walk-in trade, your supplier relationships and your margin. We take a small fee per cylinder delivered on your behalf.
