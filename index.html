What is checked before it reaches you.

Weight

Weighed in front of you

Seal

05 — Questions

Before you order.

Which areas do you deliver to? +

Kilimani, Lavington, Kileleshwa, Westlands, Riverside, Parklands, South B, Langata and Karen. We add estates as route density allows.

How is the price fixed? +

We agree a monthly rate with one vetted depot and hold it for the month, so your price does not move when the market does.

Do I need my own cylinder? +

No. We exchange on delivery: full cylinder in, empty out. If you have no cylinder at all, a refundable deposit applies.

Can I schedule a regular refill? +

Yes. Households that run out predictably can set a cycle, and we message you two days before it is due.

Is this regulated? +

LPG handling in Kenya is licensed by EPRA. Gas Poa is pursuing registration and licensing ahead of launch, and will not operate before it is granted.
